# Codessa Kernel Constitution

**Authority classification**: Constitutional  
**Version**: 1.0.0  
**Status**: Normative

This document is the single highest-authority source of normative engineering knowledge in Codessa.  
All other Kernel artifacts derive from these axioms. Generated projections are never authoritative.

---

## Axiom 1

**Statement**  
Confidence must never exceed executable evidence.

**Rationale** (informative)  
This is the foundational epistemic constraint of Codessa. Any claim, projection, or decision whose confidence exceeds the strength of the evidence that can be executed and verified is considered invalid by construction.

---

## Axiom 2

**Statement**  
There shall be exactly one authoritative source for normative engineering knowledge (the Kernel).

**Rationale** (informative)  
Multiple sources of truth produce drift, contradiction, and ungovernable evolution. The Kernel is the sole durable locus of authority. All other artifacts are projections or derived views.

---

## Axiom 3

**Statement**  
Architecture defines intent; governance defines constraints; implementation realizes behavior; verification provides proof.

**Rationale** (informative)  
These four concerns are deliberately separated so that each can evolve under clear rules without contaminating the others.

---

## Axiom 4

**Statement**  
Generated projections shall never become authoritative.

**Rationale** (informative)  
Projections (skills, configurations, manifests, documentation, knowledge graphs, etc.) exist to serve specific execution environments. Elevating any projection to authoritative status would re-introduce multiple sources of truth.

---

## Axiom 5

**Statement**  
Every abstraction must reduce overall system complexity or be rejected.

**Rationale** (informative)  
Abstractions that do not measurably simplify the system accumulate technical and governance debt. Exceptional cases require explicit, documented approval through the governance process.

---

## Axiom 6

**Statement**  
Every normative claim shall identify an enforcement mechanism and declare its implementation status.

**Rationale** (informative)  
A normative claim without a declared enforcement path is unenforceable by definition. Status must be one of: Planned | Executable.

---

## Axiom 7

**Statement**  
Normative authority flows strictly downward. No lower-authority artifact may create, strengthen, or contradict a normative requirement established by a higher-authority artifact.

**Rationale** (informative)  
This prevents authority leakage and ensures that schemas, validators, and projections cannot silently rewrite constitutional or invariant constraints.

---

## Axiom 8

**Statement**  
The Kernel shall remain independent of any specific execution platform.

**Rationale** (informative)  
The Kernel must be usable by Grok, Claude, ChatGPT, local models, CI systems, IDEs, and future platforms without modification of its normative content.

---

## Axiom 9

**Statement**  
Every normative artifact shall be traceable to its constitutional authority.

**Rationale** (informative)  
Traceability is the operational guarantee that authority lineage remains intact across the lifecycle of every normative statement.
