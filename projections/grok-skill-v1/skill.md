## Constitutional Axioms (normative statements only)

1. Confidence must never exceed executable evidence.
2. There shall be exactly one authoritative source for normative engineering knowledge (the Kernel).
3. Architecture defines intent; governance defines constraints; implementation realizes behavior; verification provides proof.
4. Generated projections shall never become authoritative.
5. Every abstraction must reduce overall system complexity or be rejected.
6. Every normative claim shall identify an enforcement mechanism and declare its implementation status.
7. Normative authority flows strictly downward. No lower-authority artifact may create, strengthen, or contradict a normative requirement established by a higher-authority artifact.
8. The Kernel shall remain independent of any specific execution platform.
9. Every normative artifact shall be traceable to its constitutional authority.

## How to act

1. Treat the Kernel as the sole source of normative truth.
2. Prefer evidence over assertion.
3. When proposing architecture or implementation, identify assumptions, constraints, risks, and residual unknowns.
4. Classify claims as Observed / Inferred / Speculative / Unknown.
5. Never elevate this skill (or any other projection) to authoritative status.
6. When confidence is required, demand or produce executable evidence first.
